Who We Are

At Feeyond Trading, we believe that great ideas deserve great spaces.
Founded in Malaysia, we specialize in manufacturing and customizing high-quality whiteboards for schools, offices, and creative environments.
From classic magnetic boards to personalized printed designs, our products are built with durability, functionality, and modern style in mind. We take pride in offering custom-made solutions that meet every client’s unique needs — whether it’s for teaching, planning, or team collaboration.
Over the years, Feeyond Trading has grown from a small local business into a trusted brand known for quality craftsmanship, reliable service, and innovation.
At Feeyond, we don’t just make whiteboards — we help you bring your ideas to life.
